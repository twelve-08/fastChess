The Urgency Gambit

Settings

How to Play

The Urgency Gambit is a fast chess variant. Checkmate the opposing king before the game clock runs out.

Quick input: Move your pointer over a square and press E to activate it. This is an alternative to clicking and is useful for trackpads.

The Goal

Protect your king and put the other king in checkmate. Check means the king is under attack. Checkmate means there is no legal move that gets it out of check. A king is never captured.

Making Moves

  • You play the color selected in Settings. White moves first unless you choose Black.
  • Select one of your pieces, then select a highlighted legal destination.
  • Pawns move forward, capture diagonally, and promote to a queen on the far side.
  • Rooks move straight, bishops diagonally, queens in either direction, knights in an L shape, and kings one square.
  • Sliding pieces cannot move through other pieces. You cannot make a move that leaves your king in check.

Clocks and Scoring

  • The main clock is the game time. When it reaches zero, the game ends and the material balance decides the result.
  • You have 5.5 seconds to make each move. If you miss the window, your opponent gains one imaginary material point. The penalty stacks and counts toward the material balance, but does not add a board piece.
  • Captured pieces are shown in the capture trays. Material values are pawn 1, knight 3, bishop 3, rook 5, and queen 9.
  • Checkmate wins immediately. If the game clock expires first, the higher material total wins; an equal total is a draw.

Modifiers

  • Time-Consuming: Captures add 1.5 seconds to the game clock.
  • Imperialist: Capturing a piece on the board edge expands the board by a small amount and adds a random piece in the capturing side's color.
  • Avenger: When a pawn reaches the far side, it becomes a queen and can revive one of your captured pieces. Choose it from your capture tray. It returns to the square where it was captured. If that square is occupied by your piece, that piece is moved to an open square; if occupied by an enemy piece, the enemy piece is captured.

Settings

Choose a theme, difficulty, side, game length, board size, and whether falling-piece physics and move effects are enabled. Beginner is the easiest CPU level; Hard is the strongest.
